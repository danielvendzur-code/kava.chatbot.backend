# Preparation and drink photography

Placeholder imagery for the advisor's method steps. Pražiareň Jolka has no photos of
brewing or of served drinks on its own site, so these stand in until the roastery
supplies its own. All are from StockSnap.io under CC0 / public domain — free for
commercial use, no attribution required.

Each was cropped to 5:4 and given the same warm grade (saturation 0.8, contrast 1.06,
24% split-tone toward the kraft palette) so the set reads as one art direction next to
Jolka's own product photography.

To swap one out: drop a replacement at the same path, 5:4, 480x384 or larger.
